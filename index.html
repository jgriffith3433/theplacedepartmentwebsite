Route

The campaign

Six chapters. The last three are sealed.

  1. Ch. 1

    Return to Sender

    Wallit wakes under a stamp. A Courier brings the notice.

  2. Ch. 2

    No Fixed Address

    A wall 205 metres out. A gate with no handle.

  3. Ch. 3

    The Clearing Floor

    Past the gate, a belt carries cleared things away.

  4. Ch. 4

    The Process Servers

    Spider Bots arrive with one paper each, and wait.

  5. Ch. 5

    Strike This Address

    The Registrar reads the ledger. Every shelf has a name.

  6. Ch. 6

    Address Filed

    One last drawer. One address to file.
