What the files say

The Department keeps one entry per thing the story has shown. Here is the whole log, in its own filing language.

Open in the game as each chapter is filed. Spoilers: this page lists them all, and the creature behind the order is not named, because nothing in the files names it.

On opening a case file

The Circle

The one filed address in the Moonlit Hollow. A thing inside it exists. A thing outside it is cleared. Slice keeps it with a scalpel knife and does not play the piano any more.

Wallit

One wallet, one wooden bat, no refunds. Stamped RETURN TO SENDER and thrown into the Hollow. No sender was found. While he stands in the circle he is on the books: he can be hurt, and he can be paid.

The Place Department

Files the things that should not exist. A filed thing has an address and is allowed to stay. An unfiled thing is cleared. The Department does not hate anyone. It only clears.

Filed in Chapter 1: Return to Sender

The Courier

An Alpha Yo Bot with a clipboard. It reads a notice of striking aloud, then delivers it to the circle. Answered with: Return to sender. The first sign that the bots work from paper.

Filed in Chapter 2: No Fixed Address

The Boundary Wall

The Hollow ends at a Department wall, 205 metres out, with a gate that has no handle. A knock is answered with the same RETURN TO SENDER stamp, pressed through from the other side. The postmark torn into the stone is half of Wallit's own.

Plated Yo Bots

A Yo Bot sent again with armour when the cheap one failed. The Plated Alpha carries a red plate stamped INVALID. Wallit says so does his refund.

Filed in Chapter 3: The Clearing Floor

The Clearing Floor

A sunken yard beyond the broken gate, lit by conveyor lamps. One batch of Yo Bots is stamped out for every unfiled thing in the Hollow. The belt carries the cleared things away: a hat, a clock, a small bell. It ends at a red lamp that is a lens, turned toward the circle.

Yo Bot Origins: the Clearing Crew

Yo Bots are the Clearing Crew. They chirp because every job closes with a report: Uploading victory means filed as cleared. Plated and Mega Yo Bots are the same unit sent again, heavier.

Bolts

A Yo Bot is held together by its pay. The bolts it drops are what the Floor owes it. Wallit has been collecting what is owed.

The Foreman

A Mega Yo Bot grown big and amber, the order held on its chest. It reads the order aloud: STRIKE. THIS. ADDRESS. It did not write it.

Filed in Chapter 4: The Process Servers

Spider Bot Origins: Process Servers

Spider Bots are not clearers. They are process servers: deliveries with one paper to hand over, who stand and wait while it is read. Six legs, because they walk the whole length of the path. The paper is an address, struck.

Slice's Address

The paper the Server offered was Slice's own. Her address was struck before Wallit came. The Server will not strike until it has been refused three times. That is how it works.

Filed in Chapter 5: Strike This Address

The Order

STRIKE THIS ADDRESS. Every copy is identical, and nobody signs it. An order that is not signed can be denied. Pressed with the RETURN TO SENDER stamp, a page shows nothing, then DENIED.

The Registrar

A gold Titan Spider Bot with a ledger held open. It keeps the Department's copies and reads the pages with every leg. Struck from the record.

Filed in Chapter 6: Address Filed

Address Filed

The order that carried the wave 30 Spider Bot was only a form, and a form can be answered. DENIED. Slice filed the circle herself. The creature behind the order is not named here, because nothing here names it.
