# The Place Department: world bible

## Areas
- **The Moonlit Hollow** — night ground around one filed circle. Mood: quiet, stamped, watched. Landmark: the circle Slice keeps. Sound: wind, distant clearing belt, stamp press on the wall in Arcade. Way in: open a case file (Campaign) or take Arcade duty.
- **The Boundary Wall** — 205 metres out, gate with no handle. Knock answered RETURN TO SENDER from the other side.
- **The Clearing Floor** — sunken yard past the broken gate, conveyor lamps, Yo Bot batches. Belt carries cleared things away.
- **The archive shelves** — every shelf a name; the Registrar reads the ledger.

## Shifts (ways in)
- **Campaign** — the story in six chapters, kept in three case-file save slots. Progress stays filed. Chapter 1 is Return to Sender.
- **Arcade** — the endless Hollow: wave defense in three acts, bosses at waves 10, 20 and 30. Separate from Campaign saves.

## Landmarks
- The Circle (filed address). The Department stamp press on the Hollow wall (Arcade). The gate with no handle. The clearing belt's red lamp-lens.

## Characters
- **Wallit** — brown leather wallet, wooden bat, RETURN TO SENDER. Filed under Money; answers to nobody.
- **Slice** — pale, patient, scalpel knife; stakes the ring; says “Noted.”
- **Yo Bot / Clearing Crew** — chirpy, paid in bolts; Uploading victory means filed as cleared.
- **Spider Bot / Process Servers** — six legs, one paper to serve.
- **The Courier** — Alpha Yo Bot with a clipboard; notice of striking (Campaign Ch. 1 / featured boss on the site).

## Life
Arcade waves keep the Hollow busy; Campaign chapters move Wallit through wall, floor, servers, Registrar and filing. Site “Choose your shift” is the duty roster before the WebGL file opens.

## Story told in play
Campaign: explore, talk, pick up, then fight — each chapter its own place. Arcade: hold the circle against the Clearing Crew. Site copy names the shift on the loading card; the Unity build should read `?mode=` when a breakingthrough task wires it.
