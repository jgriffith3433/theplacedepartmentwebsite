Privacy

What we keep on file

The Place gives an address to things that should not exist. You are not one of them, so you don't get one.

Anonymous statistics

This site and the game count what happens on them: which pages are opened, when Play is pressed, how long the game takes to load, how far a run gets, and anything that breaks. That is how we find out what to fix.

No cookies. No personal data.

No names, no emails, no accounts. Your browser keeps a random number in its local storage (not a cookie) so two visits count as one visitor. It says nothing about who you are. Clearing this site's data removes it.

No IP stored

Your IP address gives a rough country and city, then it is thrown away. It is never written down.

Kept in-house

The statistics go to Hawkes Gen, our own service, and nowhere else: no advertisers, no third-party trackers. Raw events are kept for 13 months; after that only anonymous totals remain.

Ratings

If you rate a run when it ends, the stars and anything you type are kept with that run's statistics. Don't type anything you'd want back.
